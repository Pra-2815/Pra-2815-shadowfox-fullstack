Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common dental care questions.

Can medication completely relieve tooth pain?

Medications may offer short-term relief, but long-lasting relief requires identifying and treating the root cause through professional dental care.

Does teeth scaling damage enamel?

No. Scaling safely removes plaque and tartar from teeth and below the gum line. Temporary sensitivity may occur but usually subsides.

Can fluorosis stains be removed through scaling?

Scaling can remove surface stains but not fluorosis, which is an internal stain. Cosmetic treatments may be required.

Can painful teeth be treated with fillings?

Not always. If a cavity reaches the pulp, root canal treatment or extraction may be necessary.

Is a crown necessary after root canal treatment?

A crown is recommended after root canal treatment to protect the treated tooth and reduce the risk of fracture.

Can milk teeth need root canal treatment?

Yes. A milk tooth with a deep cavity may require treatment to preserve it and prevent infection or early loss.

How can I maintain good oral hygiene?

Brush twice daily, rinse after meals, floss regularly and schedule routine dental check-ups.

What is the best age to get braces?

Braces are often effective between ages 12 and 14, although treatment timing depends on the individual.

Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?

No. Removal may be recommended when wisdom teeth are impacted, infected or causing pain or damage.

Is it important to replace missing teeth?

Yes. Replacement can help maintain bite alignment and prevent adjacent teeth from shifting.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants can restore appearance and functionality and provide a stable replacement for missing teeth.

Which toothbrush and toothpaste should I use?

A soft or medium-bristled toothbrush with a small head and non-abrasive fluoride toothpaste is recommended.

What causes tooth sensitivity?

Sensitivity can result from enamel wear, tooth decay, exposed roots or hairline cracks.

How often should I see a dentist?

A routine dental check-up every six months is recommended for preventive care.
